Privacy Policy — Fruit Sort Fridge

Effective date: 25 October 2026
Last updated: 26 October 2026

Thaemu Games ("we", "us") makes the mobile game Fruit Sort Fridge (the "Game"). This policy explains what information the Game handles, why, and what choices you have.

Contact: thaemugames@gmail.com

1. The short version

  • The Game has no accounts, no login and no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts, microphone or precise location.
  • Your puzzle progress is stored on your own device, not on our servers.
  • The Game is made for children and adults alike, so it is set up for children, for everyone: no personalised advertising, no advertising identifier, and only ads suitable for young children.
  • We use Google services for gameplay statistics, child-safe advertising and purchases. They never receive your name or contact details; what they do receive is described below.
  • The Game can remind you to come back and play. Those reminders are scheduled by your own device and sent to nobody; you can switch them off in Settings.

2. Information the Game handles

2.1 Stored on your device only

The Game saves the following to your device's local app storage. It never leaves your device, and it is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data:

  • the highest level you have completed, and whether the tutorial is done;
  • your settings (sound on or off, light or dark theme);
  • your remaining undo count and the counter that paces advertisements;
  • whether you have purchased Remove Ads (a cached copy; the store remains the source of truth);
  • whether you want play reminders, and whether you have been asked for notification permission.

2.2 Reminders (notifications)

The Game can remind you to come back and play. These are local notifications: your device schedules and shows them by itself. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else, no message is received from a server, and they work with no network connection. We cannot tell whether you saw one.

The reminder text is written into the Game and is the same for everyone, apart from the level number you had reached, which is read from your device.

Your controls:

  • Turn them off in Settings → Reminders, which cancels every pending reminder at once.
  • On Android 13 and later, notifications require your permission, and the Game asks for it only after you have finished a level. Refusing is final unless you grant it in Android settings.
  • Android's own notification settings for the Game override anything in the Game.

2.3 Gameplay statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand which levels are too hard, too easy or abandoned, the Game records anonymous events about gameplay only:

What is recorded Examples
App and session activity app opened, session started, session length
Level activity level started, completed, abandoned or skipped; level number; difficulty; number of bottles and fruit; moves played; the optimal number of moves
Tutorial activity tutorial started, tutorial completed
Advertisement activity an ad was requested, shown or failed; a rewarded ad was completed
Purchase activity a Remove Ads purchase was started, completed or restored

These events describe the Game, not you. They contain no name, email address, contact details, message content or precise location. Google Analytics for Firebase also collects standard technical information such as device model, operating system version, country-level region, language and an app-instance identifier. It does not collect the advertising identifier, and nothing it collects is used for advertising. See Google's own notice at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

2.4 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the Game shows full-screen advertisements between levels, a banner, and optional rewarded advertisements you choose to watch in exchange for undos, an extra bottle or a level skip.

Because the Game is made for children as well as adults, and does not ask anyone's age, every advertisement request is made as a child-directed request, for every player:

  • no personalised or interest-based advertising, and no remarketing;
  • the advertising identifier is never used. The Game does not even request permission to read it;
  • only advertisements rated suitable for all ages;
  • no advertisement is shown when the Game starts.

Advertising is served by Google AdMob, which receives technical information such as the IP address, device type and how ads are interacted with, to deliver, limit and measure advertisements and to detect fraud. See Google's notice at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Because every player is treated as a child, the Game does not ask for advertising consent: it only requests the limited, non-personalised advertising that needs none.

Your controls:

  • Purchasing Remove Ads stops all advertisements in the Game.
  • Purchases are made through Google Play, where a parent can require approval for every purchase.

2.5 Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Remove Ads is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. The transaction is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or receive your card number, bank details or billing address. We receive only confirmation that the purchase was made, so the Game can unlock the feature and restore it on a new device.

2.6 What we never collect

We do not collect or request your name, email address, phone number, postal address, contacts, calendar, photos, camera, microphone, precise GPS location, health data, or the contents of any file on your device.

3. Why we handle this information

Purpose Information used
Making the Game work and remembering your progress local device storage
Fixing bugs and balancing level difficulty anonymous gameplay statistics
Funding a free game through child-safe advertising ad interaction and technical data (never the advertising identifier)
Selling and restoring Remove Ads purchase confirmation from Google Play

Where the GDPR applies, our legal basis is legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)): operating, securing and improving the Game, finding which levels are too hard, and funding it with non-personalised advertising. We do not rely on consent, because children cannot give it, and we never use personal data for profiling or for personalised advertising.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone except the service providers that make the Game work:

  • Google LLC — Analytics for Firebase, AdMob, Play Billing and Play's consent tools.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect our rights, safety or property.

Information handled by Google may be processed in the United States and other countries whose data protection laws differ from your own. Google states that it uses approved transfer mechanisms, including the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. How long it is kept

  • On your device: until you uninstall the Game or clear its data.
  • Gameplay statistics: retained by Google Analytics for Firebase according to the retention period we configure, which is 2 months.
  • Advertising data: retained by Google under its own policies.
  • Purchase records: retained by Google Play as required for billing and tax purposes.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the handling of your personal information, to withdraw consent, to receive a copy of your data in a portable form, and to complain to your data protection authority.

If you are in California, you may have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know, to delete, to correct, and to opt out of the "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not sell personal information. The Game shows no personalised advertising to anyone.

Because the Game has no accounts, we usually cannot identify which data belongs to you, so the fastest way to erase everything held on your device is to clear the Game's data or uninstall it. For anything else, write to thaemugames@gmail.com and we will respond within the period required by law, and in any case within 30 days.

7. Children

Fruit Sort Fridge is made for children and adults alike. Because it does not ask anyone's age, it treats every player as a child:

  • no personalised or interest-based advertising, no remarketing, and only advertisements rated for all ages;
  • the advertising identifier is not collected, and the Game does not request permission to read it;
  • gameplay statistics are used only to improve the Game, never for advertising, and are never linked to a name, account or contact details;
  • there are no accounts, chat, messaging, user-generated content or social features;
  • purchases go through Google Play, where a parent can require approval.

We aim to comply with the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR's rules for children, and Google Play's Families policy. Apart from what this policy describes, the Game collects no personal information from anyone.

If you are a parent or guardian and want to ask about, or delete, information relating to your child, write to thaemugames@gmail.com. Because the Game has no accounts, the quickest way to remove everything on the device is to clear the Game's data or uninstall it.

8. Security

We rely on your device's own app sandbox and on Google's infrastructure, which encrypts data in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above will change, and material changes will be announced in the Game or in the Play Store listing. Continuing to play after a change means you accept the updated policy.
